Our 4-Step Crafting Journey

How raw FSC-certified timber and Tuscan leather hides are transformed into architectural art.

Phase 01

Sustainable Forest Selection

We source only mature, certified Grade-A teak and walnut logs. Each timber log is naturally air-cured for up to 24 months to normalize internal moisture content to 8%, preventing all future hairline cracks or warping.

Phase 02

Traditional Mortise & Tenon Joinery

Our master woodworkers hand-chisel traditional inter-locking joints without reliance on visible metal screws or modern synthetic adhesives. This ensures the frame expands and contracts naturally with ambient humidity for centuries.

Phase 03

Tuscan Leather & Bouclé Tailoring

Hand-selected full-grain hides from Florence tanneries undergo vegetable tanning. Custom memory-foam cores are wrapped in natural down feathers before being double-stitched by hand for ultimate ergonomic contouring.

Phase 04

Organic Oil Hand-Finishing

Every wooden surface receives seven coats of hand-rubbed organic beeswax and natural linseed oil. This yields a silky matte finish that resists liquid spills while highlighting the natural grain patina.
