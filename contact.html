Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Do you offer bespoke custom dimensions for penthouses and yachts?
Yes. Every piece in our collection can be customized down to the millimeter. Our in-house CAD architects produce 3D renders prior to wood sculpting to ensure flawless integration.
How does global white-glove delivery and assembly work?
Our white-glove logistics team handles custom crating, air freight, customs clearance, and in-residence unboxing. Two Havenwood technicians install and place the furniture in your preferred room before removing all packaging.
What guarantees are provided under your 25-Year Structural Warranty?
We guarantee against frame splitting, joinery loosening, timber warping, and structural hardware failures under normal residential usage for 25 full years.
Can I request physical leather and wood timber samples prior to ordering?
Absolutely. Contact our concierge to receive a complimentary Havenwood Sample Presentation Box featuring cut samples of Grade-A Teak, American Walnut, Italian full-grain leathers, and bouclé fabrics.
